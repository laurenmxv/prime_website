# Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization Playbook

> **What this is:** GBP is the listing that appears in Google Maps results and the Local Pack (top 3 of "near me" searches). Of every dollar of value a local business generates from Google, **~70% comes from GBP**, not the website. This playbook gets you to the top 3 of "landscaper Orlando" type searches.

> **Goal:** Top 3 Local Pack for "landscapers in Orlando" and 13+ surrounding cities within 6 months.

## The 5 GBP ranking signals (in order of weight)

1. **Reviews — quantity AND velocity AND recency** (most weight)
2. **GBP posts and photos uploaded weekly** (freshness signal)
3. **Category selection + service-area accuracy**
4. **NAP citation consistency** (name, address, phone matching across the web)
5. **Profile completeness** (all fields filled, products listed, Q&A active)

The website you have already covers #4 and #5 from the supporting side. This playbook addresses #1, #2, and #3.

## Step 2 — Review request workflow (THE BIGGEST LEVER)

You're at 28 reviews. Top-3 Orlando landscapers have 80–300+. The gap is closeable in 12 months at 5–8 reviews/month.

### A) Set up the review request text template

**Save this to Manuel's phone as a text shortcut:**

> Hi [name], it was great working on your [property/service]. If you have 30 seconds, would you mind leaving a Google review? It really helps small local businesses like ours. Here's the direct link:
> https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJq9_1XVHOq6oRIJKtmctfo-8
>
> Thanks again! — Manuel · Prime Outdoor Experts

### B) The rules of asking

- **Send within 24 hours of job completion.** Conversion drops 50% after day 2.
- **Send via TEXT, not email.** Text reply rate is 8-10x email for review requests.
- **Personalize the first sentence.** "Great working on your Heathrow estate" >> "Thanks for being a customer."
- **Ask EVERY customer.** Not just the ones who seemed happy — happy customers don't always write reviews unprompted, and the ones who seemed neutral often write the most detailed reviews.
- **Never offer a discount or gift in exchange.** Google catches it and removes the review (and may suspend the profile).

### C) Track it weekly

Open a Google Sheet titled "POE Review Tracking":

| Date | Customer | Property | Asked? | Review left? | Stars | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-05-30 | John D. | Lake Mary HOA | ✓ | ⏳ | — | sent text 4pm |
| 2026-05-31 | Maria G. | Windermere estate | ✓ | ✓ | 5 | mentioned crew by name |
| 2026-06-01 | Office park | retail center | ✓ | ✗ | — | follow-up Mon |

**Target:** 5–8 review requests sent per week. 30–40% conversion = 2–3 new reviews per week = 100+ reviews per year.

### D) What to do if a customer complains
NEVER ask an unhappy customer for a review. Instead:
- Apologize, fix the issue, document the fix in the service report
- 2 weeks later (after they've experienced the fix) send a softer request: "Hope the [specific issue] has been resolved to your satisfaction. If you're happy now, a Google review would mean a lot."

## Step 3 — GBP Posts (do weekly, takes 5 min)

Most Orlando landscapers post on their GBP 1-2 times per YEAR. Posting WEEKLY puts you in the top 5% of GBPs and gives Google a freshness signal that boosts rankings.

### Weekly cadence (every Monday, 10 min):

1. Login to https://business.google.com
2. Click **Add update** → **What's new**
3. Pick ONE of these formats:

#### Format A — "Recent work" post (use 3 out of 4 weeks)
- Upload 1–2 photos from a job completed in the last 7 days
- 100-word caption mentioning the city + service:
  > "Wrapped a full landscape refresh in Heathrow this week — new mulch, refreshed bed lines, hand-shaped boxwood hedges, and irrigation tuning. Lake Mary HOAs have a high bar; we like working to it. Free property walk-through for your community: (407) 443-4505."
- CTA button: **"Call now"** → links to phone

#### Format B — "Seasonal tip" post (use every 4th week)
- Photo of relevant landscape detail (not a person, not stock)
- Educational 80-word caption:
  > "Florida lawn tip for June: water DEEP and LESS OFTEN. Your St. Augustine wants 0.75 inches twice a week, not 0.25 every day. Shallow daily watering trains roots to stay shallow and you lose the lawn the first dry spell. Set the controller to 2 days/week, longer run times. Need an irrigation audit? (407) 443-4505."
- CTA button: **"Learn more"** → links to /services/irrigation-systems/

#### Format C — Offer post (only when you have a real promo)
- Use sparingly — fake offers degrade trust
- Only when it's true: "Free fall cleanup audit for HOA boards, Oct only"

## Step 4 — Photo uploads (THIS IS UNDERUSED)

Google rewards GBPs that upload **3–5 fresh photos per week**. Most landscapers upload zero new photos after the initial setup.

### The cadence:
- **Every Friday afternoon**, Manuel uploads 3–5 photos from that week's jobs to GBP
- Photo types to rotate:
  1. **Completed work** (after photos) — the most valuable type
  2. **In-progress** — crew at work, equipment, real action shots
  3. **Detail shots** — close-ups of hand-edged bed lines, sharp lawn corners, fresh mulch
  4. **Property exterior** — wide context shots of the finished home + landscape

### Photo quality rules:
- Daylight only (no dusk/dark photos — Google's image-quality classifier deprioritizes them)
- Landscape orientation (horizontal) — Google sizes for mobile display
- Sharp focus, no motion blur
- No people without explicit signed releases (HOA boards get nervous about this)
- Geo-tag enabled on iPhone (Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Camera → While Using)

### Upload location:
- GBP dashboard → **Photos** → click **+** → drag/drop
- Manuel can do this from his phone via the Google Business app (faster)
